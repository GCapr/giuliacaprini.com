Human Agency Lab

At Sciences Po, I am a founding member and current director of the Human Agency Lab, an interdisciplinary research group studying the social impact of AI.

The lab produces original research, data and open resources on how AI is reshaping human agency.

Mission

Its goal is to speed up the dissemination of ideas, crowdsource insights, and encourage critical examination of the challenges that fast-paced technological change poses for all of us.

Weekly newsletter

The lab also publishes a weekly newsletter aimed at researchers (but free and open to the general public) summarising AI research and news.
